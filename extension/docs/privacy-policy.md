# Rohingya Reader privacy information

The published extension privacy policy is available at
[RohingyaLanguage.org](https://rohingyalanguage.org/rohingya-reader-privacy/).
This document explains the behavior of the source in this repository.

Rohingya Reader converts Hanifi Rohingya text to Rohingyalish on your device.
It has no account, backend, analytics, remote code, or network conversion service.
Page text is processed in the active tab and retained in memory for restoration;
it is not transmitted or saved to extension storage.

When you enable **Always convert this website**, the extension stores that
hostname and its enable time in `chrome.storage.local`. Temporary permission
requests use session storage. Preferences are not synchronized to another device.
The extension requests access to an individual website only when you enable
that setting. You can remove access in the popup or Chrome's extension settings.

The popup's online-converter link opens the website in a normal browser tab when
you click it. The website has its own
[privacy policy](https://rohingyalanguage.org/privacy-policy/).

Permissions are `activeTab`, `scripting`, and `storage`, plus optional access to
websites you select. They support page conversion and your saved preferences.

For privacy questions, [contact RohingyaLanguage.org](https://rohingyalanguage.org/contact/).
